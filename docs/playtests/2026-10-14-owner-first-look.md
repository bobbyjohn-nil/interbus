## Dull, confusing or ugly

- **Map:**
  - The demand dots are always on. They should toggle, with each kind (the purple and the blue) toggled separately from a menu.
  - The dotted circles with distances on them are confusing and redundant. Remove them.
  - Text about the population follows the mouse everywhere. Show it only over a demand dot.
- **Text:** capitalisation and grammar matter everywhere.
- **3D:** a button to switch 2D and 3D is annoying. Double-click and hold to switch instead. In 3D the view cannot rotate; it always looks from south to north.
- **Finance:** confusing. It needs visuals, clearer labels and where the money goes (bus maintenance, stop upkeep). Labels should be general: "Day 0" rather than "founding capital".
- **Saves:** it should not be a tab at the bottom. Put it at the top.
- **Time:** the time system is weird. It should be:
  - minutes and days;
  - 8 days to a quarter and 4 quarters to a year;
  - 1× as real time, with faster speeds building from there.
- **Pathing:** lines in Back Bay and downtown make large loops "and other things".
- **Dropdowns:** the browser's default dropdown menus should be custom ones.
- **Building:**
  - Drawing a line as consecutive stops is the only way to build.
  - There should be a Build tab, holding:
    - a tool to place single stops;
    - the current line tool;
    - Place depot.
- **Line panel:**
  - It should show only the line's information.
  - Finances belong in Finance.
  - Route editing should be a button that opens an expanded editor, like Google Maps' trip planner, to reorder, add and remove stops.
